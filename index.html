Moonbloom

a language I made up

Hi, I'm Vix. I made up a language, and this is where I keep it.

Moonbloom is the name of the kingdom, not the language. The language itself is called lunari — from the word for "living".

English isn't my first language, so please excuse any mistakes — I'm still learning.

How it sounds

Spoken normally, it sounds calm and beautiful. Shouted, it sounds genuinely unsettling — the hard k, t and r come out sharp, and suddenly it's something you want to run from.

NE! NE FEN! DRAKIN IN HEIM!

No! Don't go! Dragons in the house!

Grammar

Pronouns

mi = I · ti = you · sa = he / she / it · min = we · tin = you (plural) · san = they

"es" = to be

It never changes, no matter who it's about.

Mi es Vix. — I am Vix.

Tense is glued onto the verb

Verbs never conjugate. -ta makes the past, -va makes the future. Present tense has no ending.

amita = loved · veyva = will see · min talta = we spoke

Negation

ne goes before the verb.

Mi ne vey. — I don't see.

Questions

Start the sentence with ka.

Ka ti tal Moonbloom? — Do you speak Moonbloom?

Plural

Add -in to the noun.

drak → drakin (dragons) · miza → mizain · vina → vinain

Word order

Subject → verb → everything else.

Mi ta vey drak in skog. — I saw a dragon in the forest.

Adjectives come after the noun

The describing word goes second, never first.

hjer varm = warm heart · stal kald = cold steel · drak mör = a big dragon

Possessives come after the noun too

Who it belongs to goes after the thing, not before it.

heim mi = my house · ami ti = your love · drak san = their dragon

The possessive words

mi = my · ti = your · sa = his / her · min = our · tin = your (plural) · san = their

Heim mi es mör. — My house is big.

Conjunctions

ve = and · te = but · rez = or

Mi ve ti. — Me and you.
Sa es mör, te hjer sa es varm. — He is big, but his heart is warm.
Ka ti mjn vatr rez fyra? — Do you want water or fire?
